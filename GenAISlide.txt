Here’s a set of **slide contents** for explaining GenAI, LLMs, Transformers, and including a concrete business example (Coca-Cola’s GenAI-powered “Create Real Magic” campaign), tailored for a non-technical business team:

## Slide 1: What is GenAI (Generative AI)?

- **Generative AI** (GenAI) refers to artificial intelligence systems that can **create new content**—text, images, code, music, and more—based on the data they've learned from.
- These models can generate *original* marketing copy, product designs, customer responses, or entire reports.
- *Business benefit*: Automates creative and repetitive tasks, speeding up workflows and enabling new ways to engage customers[6][5].

## Slide 2: How Does GenAI Work?

- GenAI relies on **advanced machine learning** and huge datasets (millions of documents, images, or messages).
- It “learns” patterns from this data, so given a prompt or question, it can produce fresh, human-like content.
- *Real-world uses*: Personalized emails, ad copy, rapid prototyping, tailored product recommendations[5][6].

## Slide 3: What is an LLM (Large Language Model)?

- An **LLM** is a specific type of GenAI that specializes in **understanding and generating human language**.
- Trained on vast amounts of text, LLMs can answer questions, summarize documents, chat, translate, or produce reports.
- *Popular examples*: GPT-4 (by OpenAI), Gemini (by Google), Claude (by Anthropic)[6].

## Slide 4: What are Transformers?

- **Transformers** are the underlying technology behind LLMs and most modern GenAI.
- Transformers use a mechanism called **attention** to understand context in sentences, focusing on the most relevant words, no matter their order.
- This makes GenAI powerful for handling complex, nuanced inputs and producing relevant, coherent output.

## Slide 5: GenAI Example in Business – Coca-Cola

- **Coca-Cola’s “Create Real Magic” Campaign**
    - Coca-Cola used GenAI to let users and marketers generate personalized branded artwork and ad creatives by simply entering text prompts.
    - GenAI models combined text prompts with Coca-Cola’s branding to create original artwork and content for global campaigns.
    - *Business impact*: Reduced campaign production time by 60% and saw double-digit increases in customer engagement—all while maintaining brand consistency at scale[5].
- *How it works*: Pre-trained GenAI models (using transformer/LLM technology) understood prompts like “a summer celebration with Coca-Cola in Paris” and produced unique, brand-compliant visuals and copy instantly.

## Slide 6: Key Takeaways

- **GenAI** = AI that creates new content, reshaping how work gets done.
- **LLMs** = GenAI models for text, answering questions, and automating communications.
- **Transformers** = The technology making all this possible by handling context and relationships in data.
- **Example**: Coca-Cola uses GenAI to reinvent marketing, achieving faster content creation and higher engagement.
